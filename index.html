The Craft

How We Build

🎨
Bleach Art

Sodium hypochlorite applied in precise patterns — stenciled, freehand, splattered — to extract color and reveal the raw fiber beneath. Every burn is permanent. Every mark intentional.

🖌️
Direct Paint

Textile-grade acrylic applied by hand. Graffiti letterforms, wildstyle tags, and character work translated from walls to wearables with full dimensional layering.

💨
Spray Technique

Actual spray cans. Actual street culture. Aerosol paint with specialty fixatives that bond to natural fibers, bringing authentic can-control to fashion.

⚡
Discharge Print

Chemical discharge removes dye from reactive fabrics, creating ghost images and high-contrast reveals impossible with traditional printing.

🔥
Heat Press Art

High-heat application of foils, transfers, and metallic accents that fuse permanently to the weave — adding depth and dimension that ordinary printing cannot achieve.

✂️
Distress Work

Controlled destruction — raw cuts, sanded patches, frayed hems — applied with artist precision to create pieces that look lived-in from day one and age into legend.

From Concept to Carry

The Process

Concept

Every piece begins with a story — a wall, a word, a feeling that demands to exist on fabric.

Source

Premium blank garments selected for fabric weight, fiber content, and dye reactivity to our techniques.

Sketch

Letterforms, compositions, and colorways roughed out by hand before a single drop of paint is applied.

Execute

The artist works the garment — sometimes for hours — layering bleach, paint, and spray until the piece speaks.

Cure

Heat setting, fixative treatment, and 48-hour cure time ensure every color holds through decades of wear.

Certify

Every piece receives a signed certificate of authenticity. One artist. One garment. One number.
